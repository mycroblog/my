- A story shows its state by props alone, with no `play`: a state only an interaction reaches gets a prop on the part that holds it, the uncontrolled start (`defaultOpen`, `defaultExpanded`, `defaultShown`) or the controlled pair (`open`, `onOpenChange`), whichever its siblings take, which changes nothing when absent; of two instances of which the viewport shows one (the frame's two Menus), the story opens the one that shows — a `play` made the catalog a test and hid states no caller could ask for — ADR-0171 §3, §4, the orchestrator's brief on #3167 — 2026-11-17 — held 0 / missed 0

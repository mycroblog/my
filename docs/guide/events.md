# Events

Events let people on a server plan a gathering: a date, where to meet, and who is coming. Events stay on the server
that holds them: only people with an account on it can see or join them, and they are never sent to other servers.

## Finding events

Choose "Events" in the navigation. "Upcoming events" lists the events you can see, the nearest first. On wide screens,
"Coming up" in the side column shows the next few, and a profile shows the events that person organizes. Nobody is
notified of a new event: you learn of one from these lists, from an invitation, or from a link someone shares.

Each event shows its date in your time zone. When the event is in another time zone, its own time and the place that
time zone is named after follow, such as "Sat, 9:00 – 11:00 PM (Tokyo)". An event whose organizers are still choosing
among candidate dates says "Date to be decided".

## Answering an event

Open an event and choose "Going", "Maybe", or "Not going" under "Your answer". The answer is saved at once, and you
can change it until the event starts. Answering "Going" or "Maybe" puts your name on the list of "Attendees", which
everyone who can see the event reads. When the event has a capacity and it is full, you can still answer "Maybe".

"Add to calendar (.ics)" saves the event as a file to import into your calendar app. It holds only the places you can
see.

## Places

An event can have a place to meet in person, an online link (a stream or a meeting on another site), and a voice chat
room on this server. By default, the place in person and the online link are shown only to the attendees the
organizers approve; until then the page says that the place is shown to approved attendees, and the place is not sent
to your browser at all. A voice chat room is shown to everyone who can see the event, and opens when it starts.

Links to maps, online meetings, and the links in a description open in a new tab and name the site they go to: they
leave this server, which never checks them.

## Organizing an event

Choose "New event" on the list of events. The editor asks for:

- **Title**, and an optional **Description** (plain text; its links become clickable).
- **Time zone of the event**, **Starts**, and **Ends**. The times are those of the event's time zone, which starts as
  yours. An event lasts at most 7 days and starts within a year.
- **Places**: "Meet in person" (a name, an address, and an optional map link) and "Online link" (a name and a URL),
  and **Who sees the place and the link**: "Only the attendees you approve" or "Everyone who sees the event".
- **Who sees the event**: everyone on this server, the organizer's followers, the people the organizer follows, mutual
  follows of the organizer, or only invited people. People you invite, and people who answered, always see it.
- **Capacity**, how many people can answer "Going"; leave it empty for no limit.

On the event's page, the organizer and the co-organizers approve attendees ("Approve" and "Revoke approval"), invite
people of this server under "Invitations", and change the event with "Edit event". The organizer alone changes who
sees the event, names up to three "Co-organizers", and cancels the event with "Cancel event", optionally with a reason
that the event then shows. A cancelled event stays readable but takes no more answers.

The server's moderators can cancel an event too; its page then says so, with their reason. Some servers let only some
people create events: when you may not, creating one says "You can't create events on this server".

If you answered "Going" or "Maybe", reminders come one day and one hour before the event starts. For every
notification of events (invitations, changes, cancellations, and reminders), see [Notifications](notifications.md).

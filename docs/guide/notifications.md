# Notifications

Choose "Notifications" in the navigation. The navigation shows how many notifications are unread
("Notifications, 3 unread").

## What you are notified of

When someone follows you, mentions you, or reacts to your posts, it shows up here. The kinds you may see are:

- Mentions, quotes, boosts, favourites, and emoji reactions to your posts. Several boosts or favourites of one post are grouped into one line ("... and 2 others boosted your post"). A reply or a boost from another server that is outside whom you let reply to or boost the post, or that waits for your approval, notifies no one of a mention (see [Posting](posting.md#other-things-to-know)).
- Follows: someone followed you, requested to follow you, or accepted your follow request.
- Approval requests: someone asked to reply to, boost, or quote your post, which waits for your approval, and the author of a post approved or declined your own reply, boost, or quote. "Review the requests" opens the list where you answer them (see [Posting](posting.md#who-can-reply-boost-and-quote)).
- New posts of accounts whose posts you chose to be notified of ("Notify me of new posts").
- A poll you took part in has ended.
- An account you follow moved to another account, and what happened to your follow.
- Voice chat: someone started a voice chat room, or invited you to one.
- Notices from the moderators of the server, the result of an appeal, and the resolution of a report you filed.
- Announcements and messages of the administrators about the server, for example when it is going to close.
- The server's maximum for keeping posts will apply to your posts, and from when (see [Deleting old posts automatically](posting.md#deleting-old-posts-automatically)).
- A post you scheduled could not be published at its time, and why, for example because the post it replies to is no longer available. The draft is kept, so you can fix it and schedule it again. This notification is never emailed.
- Moderators and administrators also get notifications about what awaits them, such as new reports and sign-ups.

Each notification links to what it is about, for example "Open post" or "Open the report".

## Reading and filtering

The page "All" lists everything, newest first. The tabs "Mentions", "Favourites", "Boosts", "Follows", and "New posts"
show one kind. Opening the page with "All" marks the notifications on screen as read; a filtered list does not. New
notifications join the list while the page is open.

## Choosing how they reach you

Under "Notifications" in the settings you choose, for each kind of notification, whether it arrives "In the app", by
"Push", by "Email", or with "Sound". A kind that you turn off in the app is not created at all, so it reaches you in no
other way either.

- **Push**: choose "Notify me on this device" to get notifications on this device even while the app is closed. Your browser asks for permission first. On an iPhone or iPad, add the site to the Home Screen and open it from there.
- **Sound**: "Play a sound for new notifications" plays a sound on this device while the app is open.
- **Email**: only on servers that send email. The link at the bottom of a notification email stops the emails of that kind ("Stop these emails"); the notifications still appear in the app. If emails to your address bounce or are marked as spam, the settings page says so ("Email delivery") and explains how to start receiving them again.

Accounts you mute do not notify you (see [Blocking, muting, and reporting](safety.md)).

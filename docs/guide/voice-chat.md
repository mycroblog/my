# Voice chat

Voice chat lets people on a server talk in rooms. A server's operators decide whether voice chat is on: when it is
off, "Voice chat" is missing from the navigation, and the page says "This server does not offer voice chat". Only people
with an account on the server can join its rooms, and joining needs a browser that supports calls, on a secure
(HTTPS) connection.

## Finding and joining a room

Choose "Voice chat" in the navigation. "Open rooms" lists the rooms you can see, each with its host and how many
people are in it. A room appears there only while it is open. A room can also be linked from a post or from a hashtag
page.

Open a room and choose "Join". The call controls then offer "Speak" and "Just listen". Your browser asks for
permission to use the microphone when you first turn it on; if you refused it, allow it in the site settings of the browser.
When you join, your name is listed for everyone who can see the room. You can be in one room at a time: joining another
room leaves the first ("Move here").

In a room, people are "Speakers" or "Listeners", and the roles are Host, Co-host, Speaker, and Listener. Use "Turn
microphone on" and "Turn microphone off" to talk, "Raise hand" to ask to speak when the room does not let everyone speak,
and "Leave" to go. To report a room, see [Blocking, muting, and reporting](safety.md).

If your connection drops, the call shows "Reconnecting...". A call also ends when you join the same room from another
device, when a host removes you, or when you and the host or a co-host block each other.

## Starting a room

"Start a room" appears for the people the server's operators allow to host rooms. The form asks for:

- **Name**, and an optional **Icon** and **Description**.
- **Hashtags**: the room shows on the pages of its hashtags.
- **Who can see and join**: "Everyone on this server", "Followers of the host", "Mutual follows of the host", or "Only invited people".
- **Who can speak without asking**: "Everyone who joins", "Followers of the host", "Mutual follows of the host", or "Only people the hosts let speak".
- **Post about the room**: a post with the link to the room, visible to everyone on this server or to your followers only.
  On a server of one person, the post about a room for everyone on this server is for your followers.

"Start the room" opens it. "Change the room's settings" edits it later, and "Invite" lets you invite a person of your
server as a listener or a speaker; they get a notification and can see the room from then on. "Share" copies the link.

## Hosting

A host and the co-hosts manage the room. "Raised hands" lists the people who ask to speak ("Allow" or "Dismiss"). The
menu of a participant offers "Let speak", "Stop from speaking", "Turn off microphone", "Remove from room", "Make
co-host", and "Make host". A person you remove cannot see or join the room again.

When the host leaves, they choose who hosts the room after them, or "End the room for everyone". A room also ends when
everyone leaves, when the host is disconnected, when it reaches the time limit of the server, or when a moderator ends
it. "Voice chat history" under "Display and notifications" in the settings lists the rooms you hosted and why each
ended.

## Notifications

You are notified when someone starts a room or invites you to one, if you have not turned these notifications off under
"Voice chats" in the notification settings.

# Voice chat rules and relayed signaling

- Validating a relayed SDP or one of its attributes: [RFC 8866](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8866), search for: Media Descriptions, `a=`, Attributes
- Validating a relayed ICE candidate: [RFC 8839](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8839), search for: `candidate-attribute`, `ice-ufrag`, `typ`

# Standard Webhooks (outgoing webhooks)

- Changing the signature, its headers, or the secret's form: [Standard Webhooks specification](https://github.com/standard-webhooks/standard-webhooks/blob/main/spec/standard-webhooks.md), search for: `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, `webhook-signature`, `whsec_`, `v1,`
- Changing the retries, the timeouts, or the automatic disabling: [Standard Webhooks specification](https://github.com/standard-webhooks/standard-webhooks/blob/main/spec/standard-webhooks.md), search for: retry schedule, exponential backoff, 410 Gone
